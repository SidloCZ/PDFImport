PREFERENCES

LOCAL FILES (file:///)

To work with local PDF files opened in browser (e.g. file:///...):

  1. Open the extensions page in your browser (e.g. opera://extensions or chrome://extensions)
  2. Find PDFImport and click Details
  3. Enable "Allow access to file URLs"
